# ITERATIONS

One row per render-look-fix round. "Worst three" are what was wrong in the image under review; "Changed" is what the next render fixed.
Renders for rounds live outside the repo (scratch); the deliverable shots are in `../preview/`.

| Round | Image reviewed | Worst three | Changed / why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | gate probe, grey boxes on a plane at 3840x2160 | (pipeline only) camera sat in front of the focus point so only sky rendered; SDFGI shadows were pure black with an ortho camera 300 m away; GPU timing read 0 | Camera now sits `+basis.z * dist` behind the focus. Render goes through a fixed-size `SubViewport`, so 1080p and 4K are exact on a 2560x1664 Retina display. `Performance.TIME_PROCESS` only refreshes once per second and `viewport_get_measured_render_time_gpu` returns 0 on Metal, so frame time is wall-clock over 60 frames (see REPORT). |
| 1 | `s01_hero_day`, first full city (roads, lots, buildings, no props) | 1) flat, washed lighting: ambient too high, glass towers read dark brown; 2) roof HVAC boxes with two black fan discs read as cartoon eyes next to the badge hexagon; 3) neon yellow-green grass, pale cauliflower trees, nothing on the street | Added overlay layer (zone plates, faction brackets, territory curtain, roof badges, power rings, congestion pulse), lamps, signals, street trees, parked cars, benches. HVAC redone as light louvred boxes with a 3.6 m clear disc around the badge. Foliage darkened, grass and terrain desaturated toward the palette ground colour. |
